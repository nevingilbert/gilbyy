# 2026-10-09 — Animals in both worlds

## Topic

The owner asked for "biome appropriate animals roaming around in both worlds": nine
species across the valley and the island, as scenery that grazes, wanders and gets out
of the way. ADR `docs/decisions/0015-wildlife.md`.

## Decisions made

- **Nine species, one or two per biome** (`SPECIES` in `wildlife.ts`):
  - valley: `deer` (meadows along forest edges, herds of 3–6, stags with antlers when
    `tone < 0.35`), `hare` (open grass, 1–2), `reindeer` (snow > 0.8, 4–8, all
    antlered), `duck` (lake water 2.5–9 m deep, i.e. 16–46 m off the shore, 3–6);
  - island: `crab` (wet sand, height 0.35–1.6), `gull` (beach, 5–10, allowed to within
    22 m of the camp instead of 40, so they stand in front of the tents), `boar` and
    `macaw` (where the dunes meet the jungle, forest 0.2/0.3–0.75), `dolphin` (sea at
    least 4 m deep within 330 m of land, with a whole 58–90 m loop over 2 m+ water).
- **Four ways of getting about** (`Way`): `walk` (graze, wander after the herd's
  `focus`, flee), `perch` (birds that sit, fly off together, circle the new `focus`,
  glide down and settle), `soar` (macaws, a function of time only) and `swim`
  (dolphins, a function of time only: under the surface, leaping 1.4 m every 5.5 s).
- **They get out of the way; nothing hits them.** They aren't physics obstacles.
  `reach(sp, speed)` grows from `calm` (parked truck) to `wary` at `ALARM_SPEED` = 12
  m/s (deer 14 → 48 m). `fleeHeading()` runs away and, if the threat is coming at it,
  off to the side of its path (factor 1.5 × how head-on), so a 30 m/s truck still goes
  by. Crabs dig in (`hide`, `tuck` → 1) within 6 m instead. A fleeing animal alarms
  herd-mates within 40 m. Threats are the truck, other players' trucks (`positions()`
  in `remote.ts` now carries `speed`) and every train carriage at `TRAIN_SPEED`.
- **They keep to their ground themselves**: `passable()` keeps them out of deep water,
  off slopes over 0.65, out of `campDist < 6`, `siteDist < 26` (yards and landmarks)
  and within 30 m of a runway, and on the sand for crabs and gulls; `go()` looks ahead,
  tries `DETOURS`, and pushes out of tree trunks and boulders. `habitat()` is stricter,
  for homes: also 40 m from the camp, 45 m from yards, 80 m from airstrips, off roads,
  the railway and courses.
- **Not shared, not counted.** Every browser places the same herds from a fixed seed
  (`placeHerds(world, seed = 20261009)`); after that each simulates its own, so no
  Realtime budget is spent. Macaws and dolphins follow the game clock, which signed-in
  players share, so friends see those alike. No miles, counts, map marks or
  achievements. `CLAUDE.md`'s "Don't add pressure" now says so.
- **Birds fly low** because the chase camera is ~8.5 m over the truck looking down and
  shows nothing higher: gulls `cruise: 6`, ducks 7, macaws 5 (±1.8). Found by
  screenshot: at 15 m the macaws were never on screen.
- **Only near herds move**: `stepWildlife()` skips herds further than `ACTIVE` (320 m)
  + `range` + `loop` from the truck; drawn only within each species' `see`.
- **The opening shot has animals** (`SHOWCASE`/`inView()`): a deer herd 135 m in front
  of the valley camp at (−810, −50); gulls 54 m and dolphins 238 m off the island camp.
- **Rendering** (`animal-models.ts`): bodies of `lump`/`block`/`stick`/`spike` pieces,
  vertex-coloured via `painted()` (now exported from `scenery.ts`), cut into `Part`s
  with a `Joint` (`body`, `head`, `antlers`, `leg`, `wing`). One `InstancedMesh` per
  part per species with `DynamicDrawUsage`, update ranges for only the slots in use,
  `frustumCulled = false`, shadows on. Gaits: `hoof` (diagonal walk, gallop with bob
  and pitch), `hop`, `scuttle`, `wing`, `swim`. Small ones are drawn bigger (`size`:
  hare 1.25, gull 1.15, duck 1.25, macaw 1.4).
- **Colours** are in `PALETTE.animal` (`palette.ts`), coats a shade cooler and darker
  than the ground; `boarCoat` was lightened to `#5c4637` after a screenshot lost them
  in the jungle shade.
- **Worked in a git worktree** on the owner's mid-session request ("use worktrees"):
  `.claude/worktrees/game-wildlife` on branch `feat/game-wildlife` (excluded locally
  via `.git/info/exclude`, not `.gitignore`). The main checkout was put back on `main`
  (e87d621), untouched.

## Open questions

- **Frame rate on real devices** with the animals is unmeasured: about 700 per world,
  a few dozen moving at once, ~20 extra instanced draw calls (plus shadows) in the
  valley and ~14 on the island.
- **Not looked at on a phone screen**, though nothing in the HUD changed.
- Macaws fly loops that ignore trees and can pass through a crown at the jungle edge.
- During the flight's film the truck is still a threat, so animals under the plane's
  path flee. Harmless, arguably plane noise.
- No sound, no splash when a dolphin leaps, no tracks or dust when a herd runs.
- Hares and crabs are small from the chase camera even at 1.25× and 1×; if the owner
  wants them noticed more, raise `size` in their bodies.
- Whether the owner wants more species (the art direction has room for, say, eagles,
  but anything higher than ~8 m is off screen).
