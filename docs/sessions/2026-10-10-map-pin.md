# 2026-10-10 — A pin on the map

## Topic

The owner asked: "u should be able to click on the map to set a pin and then the
compass takes u there." Built in the worktree `.claude/worktrees/map-pin`, branch
`worktree-map-pin` (from `origin/main` at pull request 14). No ADR: the choices are
small and are written down here and in `pin.ts`.

## Decisions made

- **One pin, the player's own, not saved.** `let pin: Spot | null` lives in the game
  loop's closure in `Game.tsx`, set only through `setPin()`. It goes when the truck
  gets within `PIN_REACH` (30 m), when the player clicks it on the map, on a flight
  (`enterWorld`), or with the page. Nothing reaches the store or Supabase; no
  migration. The game never sets one itself.
- **It can go anywhere on the sheet, fog or no fog.** It is drawn over the fog and
  shows nothing under it. That includes the hidden airstrip and the easter eggs: a
  player who knows where one is may pin it, which is not the game putting it on the
  compass. `CLAUDE.md`'s "Don't let the client say which world it's in" bullet says so.
- **A click on the sheet is for the pin; a click off it closes the map.** Before, a
  click anywhere closed it. The canvas's `onClick` stops the click reaching the
  backdrop and calls `actions.current.pin(fx, fy, px)` with the click as fractions of
  the sheet and the sheet's width in CSS pixels. M and Esc still close it. On a phone
  the only way out is tapping outside the sheet, and the hint line says so.
- **Clicking the pin takes it up; clicking elsewhere moves it.** `onPin()` counts a
  click within `PIN_GRAB` (18 CSS px, or the head's radius if that is bigger) of the
  drawn pin, from its point up to its head. The pin's proportions (`PIN_STEM`,
  `PIN_HEAD`, fractions of the sheet's width) are in `pin.ts` and `map.ts` draws from
  them, so what is drawn and what is clickable can't disagree. So moving a pin a
  very short way takes two clicks.
- **The compass keeps one mark.** `docs/art-direction.md` asks for a nearly absent
  HUD, so the pin borrows the existing mark instead of adding a second. `markFor()`
  in `pin.ts` has the order: the next flag of a course first (unchanged, in `aim`);
  nothing while a course counts in or a convoy gathers; then a friend's call to a
  convoy or race; then the pin; then the first-time guidance. A call outranks the pin
  because it is short-lived and its toast says "Follow the compass to join"; the pin
  comes back when the call ends, and stays on the minimap meanwhile. The pin outranks
  the guidance because single player starts the guidance again every visit, so the
  other way round the pin would look broken to anyone not signed in.
- **The mark shows what it's following.** Leading to the pin it is round, in
  `PALETTE.map.pin` (a berry, `#a53b72`, used by nothing else on the sheet) with a
  paper outline; otherwise the yellow diamond as before. Set from the loop as inline
  styles, guarded by `mark.dataset.pin`, because the mark's element is a new one each
  time the compass is shown again.
- **The minimap shows it too.** Within reach, the pin itself; further off than
  `radius * 0.9` (432 m), a bead on the rim on the way to it (`onMini()`).
- **The newcomer's line doesn't lie.** `Goal` has an optional `pinned` wording and
  `currentGoal(done, online, pinned)` uses it: with a pin down the garage line reads
  "Find a garage. Take up your pin and the compass leads to one."
- **Checked in the browser** at 1280x800 and 375x812, valley and island, day and
  night: the pin lands under the click, clicking its head takes it up, clicking off
  the sheet closes the map, the mark and the minimap follow it, and reversing 60 m to
  a pin took it up and put the guidance's diamond back.
